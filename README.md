# chocolate-wrapping-machine
In the chocolate manufacturing industry, efficient and consistent packaging is critical to maintain product quality, enhance shelf life, and improve visual appeal to attract customers. A chocolate wrapping machine is essential to automate and streamline the wrapping process, ensuring that each piece of chocolate is wrapped uniformly, hygienically, and at high speed, reducing labor costs and minimizing errors.

## Overview

The Chocolate Wrapping Machine was developed as a first-year Engineering Exploration project at KLE Technological University. The project aimed to understand the complete engineering product development cycle-from identifying a problem to designing, modelling, fabricating, programming, testing, and demonstrating a working prototype. This prototype integrates mechanical engineering with embedded electronics by using an Arduino Mega 2560 to control multiple DC motors through motor drivers, simulating an automated chocolate wrapping process.
