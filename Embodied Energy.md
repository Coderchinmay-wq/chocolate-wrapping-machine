## Formula Used

- Volume of cuboid = length x breadth x height 

- Volume of cylinder = pi x r^2 x h

- Total volume = Volume of individual x Quantity

- Density = mass/volume

- mass = density x volume

- Total mass = mass of individual item x quantity

- Net embodied energy = unit embodied energy x total mass
