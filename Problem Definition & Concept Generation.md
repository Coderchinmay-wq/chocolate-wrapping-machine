# Problem Definition
## Need Statement
In the chocolate manufacturing industry, efficient and consistent packaging is critical to maintain product quality, enhance shelf life, and improve visual appeal to attract customers. A chocolate wrapping machine is essential to automate and streamline the wrapping process, ensuring that each piece of chocolate is wrapped uniformly, hygienically, and at high speed, reducing labor costs and minimizing errors.

## Basic Survey and requirements
| SL.NO | Observation from surveys                     | Requirements                                                                 |
|-------|---------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1     | Materials to be used                         | Materials used to build must be easy to use and replaceable. As it is used in large scale, it is important. |
| 2     | Cost                                         | Machine should be built with minimum budget.                                 |
| 3     | Accuracy of chocolate wrapping               | It should be accurate in cutting perfect edges after wrapping and should wrap empty wrappers.                 |
| 4     | Less human interference                      | In chocolate wrapping process, humans have only work to store the chocolate to ensure hygiene.                |

# Concept Generation
## Establishing functions of machine
1.	Store the raw chocolates
2.	Store the wrappers
3.	Dispense the raw chocolates in conveyor belt
4.	Move the chocolates to wrapping unit
5.	Dispense the wrappers to wrapping unit
6.	Sense the chocolates on wrappers
7.	Twist the chocolates with accurate measurements
8.	Twisting the side ends of chocolates
9.	Dispense the chocolates to storing unit
10.	Store the processed chocolates
